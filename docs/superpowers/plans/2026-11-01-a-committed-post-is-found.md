## What the reading found that the register did not know

1. **The exercise's own oracle counted a vote.** `ctl/counts.sh` printed `unindexed=7 baseline=1 difference=6`; the baseline is the vote V1 (`HARNESS.md:50`), a `post.accepted` event of a kind the index does not hold. A check that enrolled "every `post.accepted` digest the index lacks" would have counted that vote, every unplaceable post (`NoFeatures`, G51 decision 2) and every post whose index step is running at that instant: a false `1 committed post(s) not yet in the vector index` on a healthy Forum every time it ran (G54 finding 6). The rule is therefore the reconcile's own (discussion kinds the model can place), and the probes' oracle writes the kind set and the placeable condition itself and subtracts nothing (trap 97 below).
2. **The absence rule exists and is shaped as a start-up pass.** `ReconcileByAbsenceAsync` indexes as it scans and returns its first failure (`EmbeddingIndexer.cs:73-122`), so a scan that meets one failing post leaves every later post unknown and the count at zero. The sweep enrols instead (G54 decision 1) and the existing per-entry ladder indexes; the start reconcile and the overflow pass keep indexing, from the same extracted rule (two predicates for one question is trap 96).
3. **Keying the obligation on the status would miss a door.** The middleware ignores a request whose own token is cancelled (`StoreFaultsMiddleware`, the `when` clause), so a client that disconnects while the append is in flight answers nothing and may leave a committed post; no status or problem type exists to key on. The mark is set at the route's exit from `PersistAsync` by any exception (G54 decision 2), which also covers the two types the ruling named. **This deviates from the ruling's letter** in one place: a `curia/log/unreadable` raised before PERSIST (AUTHORIZE's reads, a gate) does not owe a check, because R5.23 and R8.82 publish "nothing was recorded" beside it and an owed check beside that sentence would contradict it; a `log/unreadable` raised inside `PersistAsync` does.
4. **One check at the next tick is wrong by PR 1's own M3.** The late commit (1.05 s and 2.06 s after the `503`) means a check at +1 s can find nothing and be right, and a check that then clears the obligation never finds the post (until the floor). The obligation outlives the window: repeated every 60 s while owed, cleared only by a check that began at least 60 s after the latest exit (G54 decision 2, R8.83). The 60 is a figure with margin, not a bound; the floor is the bound. *(Amended, ruling 10: at Npgsql's default 30 s command timeout the Forum answers `503` at 30.2 s and a commit stalled at `COMMIT` lands the stall less 30.2 s after the answer, measured; there is no 2 s cancel wait at a `COMMIT` stall. A 60-second window is therefore outrun from a stall of about 90 s, and the plan's "about 32 s" was wrong.)*
5. **The ruling's boolean cannot say when.** `vector_sweep_owed` becomes `vector_check_due_at`, an instant or `null` (G54 decision 4); the second member, `vector_checked_at`, gives "none pending" an age (finding 7 of G54), which the ruling did not have and which is the only way a reader can tell a maintained count from a forgotten one.
6. **The success reset needs a limit the ruling did not state.** Every healthy post's index step is a success; unlimited, a reset on success retries a poison entry (`retry-event-not-found`, which no recovery changes) at every tick of every busy minute. At most once per 5 seconds (G54 decision 7, R11.69 (rev.)); and a post the model cannot place is not a success.
7. **A watermark is sound and is not built.** `PostgresEventStore.cs:150-175` takes one transaction-scoped lock for the whole log "so seq order and commit order are the same order" (R6.47), so a scan that resumed from a high-water mark could not miss a lower sequence that becomes visible later. That makes a watermark safe if the full scan is too dear; Task 0 (M4) measures the scan before anyone writes it, and without the R6.47 lock it would be wrong (a late commit lands below it).
8. **The harness is not yet in `tools/`.** The rulings ask for the controller's `ctl/` (the commit-cut proxy, the grant windows, `counts.sh`) to be promoted to `tools/` before this PR's Task 0; `tools/` holds `Curia.Differential`, `differential-oracle`, `GenerateEnvelopeFixtures` and `spec-checks` only. Task 0 step 0 is that promotion, by the controller (not writable by the architect), scrubbed of every private path and host name, in its own commit.
9. **Three allocations of the figure 60, two quantities.** R10.93's client wait (the `unknown` line's 60 s, PR 1), the Forum's repeat interval while owed and its settle window (G54 `SweepRepeat`, `SweepSettle`) are three constants with different meanings and one value. They are published and read separately (a probe reads each from its own paragraph); no probe asserts that they are equal, because a future edit that moves one for its own reason must not be a build failure of the other (and a probe that asserted equality would restate the code, vacuity question 4).

## What an agent reads, and when

Judged as the one who reads it. An agent whose `ask` is answered `503 curia/store/unavailable` and who then (or any agent, any time) runs `curia search`:

| when | `vector_check_due_at` | the line says |
|---|---|---|
| the Forum is healthy, last check at start or at the floor | `null` | `vector  none pending; no check is owed; the Forum last compared its log with the vector index at 2026-11-01T10:00:00.0000000+00:00` |
| the instant the route's `catch` ran, before any tick (CPF2): the `503` has been answered | the next tick | `vector  none counted as pending; a check is owed (a post the Forum answered with a fault may be committed and not yet in the vector index: found by words, not yet by meaning) and next runs at <the tick>; the Forum last compared its log with the vector index at <T>` |
| the first check ran (about 1 s later) and found the post, and it is stored | now +60 s | the same owed form; the post is found by meaning (the anti-join is empty), the obligation stands because the late-commit window has not closed |
| the first check found nothing (the commit has not landed: PR 1's 1.05 s to 2.06 s) | now +60 s | the same owed form; at the repeat the post is enrolled, counted (`1 committed post(s) not yet ...; and a check is owed ...`) and stored |
| a check began at least 60 s after the last exit and completed | `null` | the first row's form with the new instant |
| the log or the index could not be read during a check | the next attempt (5 s, doubling to 60 s) | the owed form with a later instant; an instant in the past beside an owed check is how the reader sees a check that is failing |

The line never says `none pending` between the second row and the last, and never says it for a member the Forum did not state. "Found by words, not yet by meaning" is true throughout: the lexical channel reads the log and finds the post at once (the exercise's scout found its own committed-`503` question by title, body and tag). The agent's act is nothing: it does not resend, and if meaning matters to it, it searches again after the instant the line states.

## Rulings (decided, not asked)

1. **One absence rule; the check enrols, the catch-up indexes** (G54 decision 1). The start reconcile and the overflow pass keep indexing from the extracted rule; the check enrols what the rule returns with its sequence. A scan that cannot complete leaves what it enrolled enrolled (a post known to be absent is never un-known) and moves nothing else.
2. **The obligation is created at the route's exit from PERSIST by any exception, before the exception leaves** (G54 decision 2). One `catch`, one call site; a source scan holds that `Owe` has one call site (CPF28), so PR 3 changes it and cannot add a second.
3. **The check's rule skips what the code legitimately never indexes**: kinds the index does not hold (the route's `IsDiscussion` predicate and the reconcile's own `SearchProjector.TryRead`), posts the model cannot place (remembered by digest, bounded 4,096), posts whose index step is running in this process. The probes' oracle is the opposite artifact: SQL, with its own kind set, over `post_embeddings`.
4. **`vector_check_due_at` is a time**; `vector_checked_at` is the instant the last completed check *began*, taken from the clock port before the scan (CPF21 makes the scan take fake time, so began and completed differ and a check that stamps its completion is red).
5. **The success reset is rate-limited to once per 5 seconds and excludes `NotApplicable`** (G54 decision 7), because a poison entry must not be retried at every tick of a busy minute (CPF17) and a post with no features proves nothing about the store (CPF16).
6. **The store sentence, the act line and the `201` receipt are not edited.** An owed check is visible in the search and not in a refusal's own words; PR 3 amends R11.72's sentence to "not confirmed" and must not collide with a PR that also edited it (CPF29 pins both byte for byte as G53 published them).
7. **The 60-second settle window, the 60-second repeat and the 5-minute floor are provisional figures**, published in `R8.83 words`, read by `PublishedCheckWords` one at a time; none is claimed as a bound. The floor is what bounds a post that lands later than the window (G54 non-property b).
8. **The cost is measured before it is accepted** (M4): one full scan and one read of the index's digests per check. A decision rule, written before the measurement so it cannot be fitted to it: if one scan at 50,000 events takes more than 2 s of the Forum's pool, the plan is amended in place with the floor interval raised or the watermark (ruling in finding 7, with M5) added; if not, the full scan stands.
9. **Each of Groups A to D is its own commit.** Group A (the check) goes first; C (the reset) is independent; B (the route and the members) needs A; D (the words) needs B. The seam, the red probes and the G51-span pointer edit are their own commits.
