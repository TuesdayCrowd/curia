## What the reading found that the rulings did not know

The rulings decide five remedies in a sentence each. Reading the readers they bind found eleven facts they did not have; each changes a detail and six change how the work is built.

1. **Item 45's clause is the truth on the revision route and the title is shared, so "the route words the 403" cannot be built as ruled.** `table-10/own-resource-only` is the reason for every `(own)` parenthetical (`AccessPolicy.cs:84-91`), titled "this is not your resource, or it does not exist" (`:219`). The revision route discharges it with `RevisesOwnPost` (`ForumEndpoints.cs:906-907`) and cannot tell a post that is another's from a `prev` that names nothing; `RevisionOwnershipTests` asserts "does not exist" in that title on purpose (R8.64, ruling 7). The flag listing settles existence first (`:1380-1385`) and then says the same words of a post it has found. R7.24 (rev.) says a title is derived from the decision's reason and from nothing else, so the route cannot choose its own. The remedy is a second reason, issued only by a route that knows (G50 decision 1, R7.30); dropping the clause would make the revision route assert what it has not looked up, and the PDP-driven fact `R7_24_TheCausesTheErrataListsAreTheReasonsThePdpGives` counts eight reasons the PDP gives and must keep counting them.
2. **Item 42 cannot be built from the receipt as it is.** The ruling asks the first-acceptance receipt to "carry the `relation` line", and `replaced_relation` is `null` on a first acceptance (`ForumEndpoints.cs:2041-2042`, `:2057-2059`): there is nothing to state it from. Both clients decline to read the post before accepting it (`Program.cs:748-750`; `WriteTools.ResolveAsync` reads nothing, `:285`). The Forum has both facts at the receipt (`isRevision` `:1984`, `chainRoot` `:1935`), so the receipt gains two members (R8.80). It is a wire change and additive, and it is the one Forum change in the PR besides the 403's reason.
3. **The obvious name for the second member is taken, with another meaning.** The log's `answer.accepted` event already has a member `answer_id`, and it is the post accepted (a revision's own id for a revision: `AcceptanceProjection.cs:31` and a dozen SQL oracles in `tests/Curia.Api.Tests`). A receipt member of that name meaning "the answer whose text this is" would be one word with two meanings, the shape of trap 57. It is `text_of`, and `post_id` on the receipt is the event's `answer_id`.
4. **Item 43's walk is down and unsigned, and the CLI reads nothing to walk from.** `curia answer` (`Program.cs:255-320`) takes `--board` and `--parent` as given and never fetches the question; "the CLI's receipt states the standing it was given" has no standing to state. The tool already reads the question (`WriteTools.cs:196`). R11.55's walk (`:228-267`) goes up, by the author's signed `parent`, and confirms each hop by the Forum's `superseded_by` listing the revision's recomputed digest; the receipt's walk goes down by `superseded_by` alone and can confirm a hop by the digest it recomputes and by the signed `prev` of the post it fetched. A fork is reachable (the revised-once gate, R8.77, refuses a second revision while the first is served; withholding the first lets a second in, and restoring the first makes two served), so FORKED is not a dead arm.
5. **Item 38 is `curia_read`'s alone and only a hand-signed revision reaches it.** `curia thread` takes a root and walks nothing (`Program.cs:965-981`); `curia browse` and the screen print the post's `parent` as a row and walk nothing (`PassageHeader.cs:134`, `Passage.cs:128`). The clients sign a revision with `Parent = post.PostId` (`WriteTools.cs:160`; `curia revision --parent` names the post revised, `Program.cs:343`), so the parent walk reaches the root of every chain they make, to the walk's bound of 64 (`ForumTools.cs:56`). The thread the Forum lists for a revision is its chain root's (`PostProjection.cs:118-144`, R9.48).
6. **Nothing upward is in a member.** `superseded_by` lists the revisions of a post (`CorrectionIndex.For`, `:83-86`) and `fork` the other revisions of the post a revision revises (`ForkOf`, `:94-109`; both in `src/Curia.Application/Projections/CorrectionIndex.cs`, not `PostProjection.cs`, which holds the thread at `:118-144`): down and sideways.
