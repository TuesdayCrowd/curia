## What the reading found that the rulings did not know

The rulings decide five remedies in a sentence each. Reading the readers they bind found eleven facts they did not have; each changes a detail and six change how the work is built.

1. **Item 45's clause is the truth on the revision route and the title is shared, so "the route words the 403" cannot be built as ruled.** `table-10/own-resource-only` is the reason for every `(own)` parenthetical (`AccessPolicy.cs:84-91`), titled "this is not your resource, or it does not exist" (`:219`). The revision route discharges it with `RevisesOwnPost` (`ForumEndpoints.cs:906-907`) and cannot tell a post that is another's from a `prev` that names nothing; `RevisionOwnershipTests` asserts "does not exist" in that title on purpose (R8.64, ruling 7). The flag listing settles existence first (`:1380-1385`) and then says the same words of a post it has found. R7.24 (rev.) says a title is derived from the decision's reason and from nothing else, so the route cannot choose its own. The remedy is a second reason, issued only by a route that knows (G50 decision 1, R7.30); dropping the clause would make the revision route assert what it has not looked up, and the PDP-driven fact `R7_24_TheCausesTheErrataListsAreTheReasonsThePdpGives` counts eight reasons the PDP gives and must keep counting them.
2. **Item 42 cannot be built from the receipt as it is.** The ruling asks the first-acceptance receipt to "carry the `relation` line", and `replaced_relation` is `null` on a first acceptance (`ForumEndpoints.cs:2041-2042`, `:2057-2059`): there is nothing to state it from. Both clients decline to read the post before accepting it (`Program.cs:748-750`; `WriteTools.ResolveAsync` reads nothing, `:285`). The Forum has both facts at the receipt (`isRevision` `:1984`, `chainRoot` `:1935`), so the receipt gains two members (R8.80). It is a wire change and additive, and it is the one Forum change in the PR besides the 403's reason.
3. **The obvious name for the second member is taken, with another meaning.** The log's `answer.accepted` event already has a member `answer_id`, and it is the post accepted (a revision's own id for a revision: `AcceptanceProjection.cs:31` and a dozen SQL oracles in `tests/Curia.Api.Tests`). A receipt member of that name meaning "the answer whose text this is" would be one word with two meanings, the shape of trap 57. It is `text_of`, and `post_id` on the receipt is the event's `answer_id`.
4. **Item 43's walk is down and unsigned, and the CLI reads nothing to walk from.** `curia answer` (`Program.cs:255-320`) takes `--board` and `--parent` as given and never fetches the question; "the CLI's receipt states the standing it was given" has no standing to state. The tool already reads the question (`WriteTools.cs:196`). R11.55's walk (`:228-267`) goes up, by the author's signed `parent`, and confirms each hop by the Forum's `superseded_by` listing the revision's recomputed digest; the receipt's walk goes down by `superseded_by` alone and can confirm a hop by the digest it recomputes and by the signed `prev` of the post it fetched. A fork is reachable (the revised-once gate, R8.77, refuses a second revision while the first is served; withholding the first lets a second in, and restoring the first makes two served), so FORKED is not a dead arm.
5. **Item 38 is `curia_read`'s alone and only a hand-signed revision reaches it.** `curia thread` takes a root and walks nothing (`Program.cs:965-981`); `curia browse` and the screen print the post's `parent` as a row and walk nothing (`PassageHeader.cs:134`, `Passage.cs:128`). The clients sign a revision with `Parent = post.PostId` (`WriteTools.cs:160`; `curia revision --parent` names the post revised, `Program.cs:343`), so the parent walk reaches the root of every chain they make, to the walk's bound of 64 (`ForumTools.cs:56`). The thread the Forum lists for a revision is its chain root's (`PostProjection.cs:118-144`, R9.48).
6. **Nothing upward is in a member.** `superseded_by` lists the revisions of a post (`CorrectionIndex.For`, `:83-86`) and `fork` the other revisions of the post a revision revises (`ForkOf`, `:94-109`): down and sideways. A chain-root walk is therefore one `recheck` per hop and a second copy of R8.75's rule in the client; a Forum member would be one read. The decision (G50 decision 5) is neither, with a conditional that Task 0's M4 settles.

## Rulings (decided, not asked)

1. **A second reason, and only the route that knows issues it (item 45).** `table-10/own-resource-found`, passed by `ListPostFlagsAsync` as `resourceKnownToExist: true` to `Discharge`, titled "Not permitted: this resource exists and is not yours". The default is the hedge, so a route that forgets says too little; a call-site fence holds that exactly one site passes `true` and a second must be a reasoned row that shows the existence check (RLW8). The revision route's reason, title and status are untouched (RLW5, which is green on the seam and says so). **Existence:** no new bit, because the status already partitions ids (`404` no post, `403` a post) for every credentialed caller and the log's inputs are public (R6.51); RLW4 holds the partition against the events table by SQL and not against the old behaviour, and Task 0's M1 tabulates it for six kinds of id before the change. Where the single read hides an id (a vote's), this route's status already told it and the plan leaves that as recorded non-property (e).
2. **The wire change is two members and no more (item 42).** `post_kind` and `text_of`, always present, `201` and `200`, built from the post and its chain root the route already holds. Told only after the ownership check (the receipt exists only after it: a non-asker's `403` carries neither, RLW18). `text_of` is stated for an answer the Forum does not serve, to the asker, as `replaced_answer` is (R8.71): the asker's thread, not a stranger's, and the id is the one thing the asker's own call named. The reader is strict: an answer whose `text_of` is not its own `post_id`, a revision whose is, a kind that is neither, a non-string, are a malformed receipt (RLW19); an absent member is `kind NOT STATED`, never `answer` (FRLW20 is the first patch that reads silence as an answer).
3. **One definition of the words for a digest-only entry, and `recheck` keeps its own clause (item 41).** The words are `not served; recheck of its digest says whether it is withheld`, written once in `Supersession` and used by `Revisions` (every form that lists successors), `AnswerStanding`, `ForumTools.AcceptedLine` and `RevisionRefusal`'s arm. `Revisions` takes whether to carry the pointer; `CitationLines` calls it with the pointer off because `UnservedBranches` already states it for the same digest (R10.89), from the same constant, so `recheck`'s line states it once and its existing facts stay green. The reader never says `withheld` itself (R10.91): a digest-only entry is the Forum's word that the post is not served, and `recheck`, which asks the Forum, is the one that can say more. No member, no post id, no count: the ruling is the architect's and the plan holds it (FRLW13 and FRLW14 are the first patches of the auditor's wish). The definition sentence is added to every description and help that prints such an entry, data-driven over the tools whose descriptions contain "not served" (RLW15), so a tool added later is red until it carries it.
4. **The pointer is true, and Task 0 shows it.** For a revision, `IsServed` is `PostKinds.IsServedToReaders(kind) && servable(postId)` and a revision is always of a kind readers are served, so "not served" means "not servable" and `CitationCheck.Resolve` answers `withheld` for exactly it (`CitationCheck.cs:107-108`). For an accepted answer the same predicate holds. Trap 57 is the hazard (one word, three predicates), so RLW12 puts the Forum's own `recheck` answer, from a real withhold, in the same fact as the pointer, and Task 0's M5 asks the same of an automated quarantine where one is reachable.
5. **No chain-root walk and no Forum member for item 38; the reader says what it knows (decision 5 of G50).** The two failure lines are split by cause: `NOT READ ... could not be read` stays for a thread the Forum could not be asked for or answered with nothing, and `NOT LISTED` is the thread that was read and does not list the post. For a post of kind `revision` both lines, and the `CUT` line, add `RevisionPlacementNote`: the Forum places a revision by its correction chain and not by the `parent` its author wrote, the digest the post's signed `prev` states (only where the post's own passage verified; the preamble is built after the passages, so the note cannot name a `prev` read from a document that did not verify), and that `curia_recheck` of that digest gives the post to read. Reasons, each from the reading: `superseded_by` and `fork` carry nothing upward (finding 6); the clients' revisions have `parent` as their predecessor, so the walk as it stands reaches every honest chain's root; a walk by `prev` is a `recheck` per hop and a client copy of R8.75; a Forum member buys one read for a case only a hand-signed client reaches. **The conditional, settled by Task 0's M4:** if an honest chain made by `curia_revise` to depth 8 does not read as `thread root=<the question>` under the unmodified walk, or the chain's reads are not the `parent` hops the reading says, the ruling flips (a walk by `prev`, confirmed by `superseded_by`, as R11.55's pointer confirms its hops) and the plan stops and reports before building it: it would be a different PR. The wording split is built either way.
6. **The walk down is advisory, before the act, bounded at eight, and the same in both clients (item 43).** `QuestionChain.WalkAsync(client, question, ct)` follows `superseded_by` from the question the client already holds: nothing for an empty or absent list; for one successor with a post id, `GetPostAsync(id)` (marking none, as the refusal path reads), then three keyless checks on the served document (the digest recomputed from its canonical form equals the digest the list named, its signed kind is `revision`, its signed `prev` equals the previous post's recomputed digest), then its own list; at most `WalkBound` (8, the constant R11.55 uses) such reads; stopping at an empty list (the latest), a list of two or more (FORKED, branches named, none followed), a successor with no post id (R10.91's words), a read that fails or a check that does not hold (fixed words, no value the Forum or an author wrote), a post already read, or the eighth read spent with a successor listed (`NOT WALKED`, the last post reached named). It is a walk of the Forum's word: no JWKS fetch, no signature check, the line says so, and the answer is never stopped by it (RLW23, RLW28). Rejected: after the post (a walk that throws would tell an agent its recorded answer failed); verifying each hop (R11.55's refusal path does, because it reads the author's word upward; the receipt reads the Forum's word downward and recomputes what it can); a bound of 64 (the receipt is a note, and eight posts is the refusal's price for the same chain). **The CLI makes one added read** of `--parent` (marking none, unverified), advisory: `question  NOT CHECKED (...)` where it fails, and the post is sent all the same (the Forum refuses a bad parent in its own words, R8.70). The words are one definition, `Supersession.QuestionChainStanding`, built on `QuestionCore` so the receipt and the refusal cannot word one list differently (R11.62's rule). A hedge is not the whole sentence (trap 54): where the walk read the chain the line says what it read.
7. **A wording split is not a rename of an existing fact (item 38).** The facts that pin `NOT READ` and "did not include" for a thread read without the post are rewritten to the new line, not loosened to `Contains("NOT")`; the facts that pin it for a thread that could not be fetched are controls and stay (RLW36).
8. **The receipt line is R10.87's sentence from one definition.** `AcceptWording.ReceiptKind(kind, postId, textOf)` is called by `Program.WriteAcceptance` and `WriteTools.ResolveAsync` and by nothing else writes the line (RLW21's IL fence); it composes `Passage.AcceptedRevisionStatement`'s fixed words (the one definition R10.87 made) rather than copying them, so the receipt cannot drift from the thread's own line. Every id is a display literal.
