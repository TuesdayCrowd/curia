6. **The inbox cursor is not bound to its query, and that is stated, not left.** [Re-examined after the sixth exercise and PR 2c's trap 39: stands; see ruling 12.] It is a keyset position `(score, seq)`; the inbox passes no text, so every hit scores 0 and the cursor is a sequence (`LexicalSearch.Search` scores 0 for an empty term list; to be confirmed at T0 by decoding a live cursor). The inbox's counts are the questions counted, and the test's own record of what it signed is the oracle.** The defect is that two readings of one sentence agreed: `InboxSelector` computed the right figure over the whole corpus, the route recomputed a figure "scoped to the filters" as a remainder, and every fixture had a page that held the whole open set, so the remainder and the count were equal. 12. **(Amendment.) The inbox cursor's non-binding stands, for a stated reason and with one conditional.** Trap 39's shape was an offset into a list that the request had shaped (`limit` windowed it), so the same offset meant different posts. The pager's lead that a forged position gives an empty last page is about search's offset cursor and is not this route's.
