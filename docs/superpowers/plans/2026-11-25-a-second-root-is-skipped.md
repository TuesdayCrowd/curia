## What this is

**The defect as filed (item 299).** A second `agent.enrolled` row in a genuine agent's stream makes the agent's token request answer `500 server_error`: the credential state machine has no `(Active, SuccessfulEnrollment)` transition, so `CredentialLifecycle.Project` fails and `TokenEndpoint` refuses with `OAuthRefusal("server_error", CouldNotIssue)` at the `PostureOf` call. The closure the register states: a second root in a stream that already holds one is skipped and counted.

**What reading found that the item's text does not say (each is a Task 0 measurement, not yet a result).**

1. **The lifecycle fold already ignores a later root, silently, and the readers already fail it.** `LifecycleFold.Apply` returns with no row for any `agent.enrolled` after the stream's first (R6.64: "a later `agent.enrolled` is not a row and binds nothing"; the Rust `honoured` fold's `ENROLLED` arm does the same). The author-chain readers, `LifecycleCheck.Folder.Enrolled` and `lifecycle.rs`'s `Fold::enrolled`, **fail** an `agent.enrolled` that is not the chain's first with `broken-link` (`Count != 0`). So the Forum says "no row" and a reader says "broken link" about one entry, and the lifecycle corpus holds **no vector with a second `agent.enrolled`** (read: a scan of the 88 lifecycle and 61 honoured cases finds exactly one case, `honoured/second-enrolment-then-binding`, and it is a Forum-fold case). The reader's branch is pinned by nothing.
2. **The 500 is not in the lifecycle fold at all.** The standing is a **second, separate fold**: `VerifiedLog.Standings()` folds `AgentStandingProjector` over `HonouredEvents`, which is the log less the *operator-act* rows R6.67 skips (`BuildHonoured`). A lifecycle-skipped row never leaves that feed, so making the lifecycle fold skip a later root would **change what is counted and nothing the token endpoint reads**. The closure has to land in the feed the standing reads, and the plan's first job is to say so before anyone writes a fold change that leaves OAS39 green. This corrects the enrolment plan's ruling 10 ("the lifecycle fold, its three readers"): the readers need **no code**, only vectors.
3. **The standing is keyed by the payload's `agent_id`, not by the aggregate** (`AgentStandingProjector.ApplyEnrollment`: `Str(fields, AgentIdField)` then `For(builders, agentId)`). A well-formed `agent.enrolled` row appended in **another identity's stream** that names the victim reaches the victim's standing through `Standings()` (the token endpoint's read) and **not** through `StandingOf(agent)` (the enrolment route's read, which filters honoured events by aggregate first). The lifecycle fold already skips that row in the other stream (`statement-mismatch`, step 4) and counts it there. So today: the same forged row is skipped by one consumer, honoured by two, and the two honouring consumers disagree. If this reads right, item 299's harm is reachable without touching the victim's stream, and it also lets such a row date the victim's tenure (`EnrolledAt ??=` takes the first), which is item 293's backdating with no write to the victim's own stream. **Read, not run: Task 0 S2 and S3 decide it.**

**The rule, decided (ruling 1).** The standing, and every consumer of it, reads an `agent.enrolled` row only if the lifecycle fold honours it as the root of its own stream. Every other `agent.enrolled` row is skipped and counted: one that is not its stream's first (whether or not the first was honoured), a first that is not an object or whose `agent_id` is not its stream's, and, by the same decision, a row in one stream naming another agent. One decision function, called from both sites that need it.

**Class:** a rule decided at one layer and read at another. The fold decided the row ("no row") and a different fold, keyed differently, read it anyway: errata G68's R5.24 sentence ("the fold and the consumers answer different questions") and item 299 are the same shape. The probes this plan needs are the kind the project has paid for: vectors whose verdicts are written from the construction, and probes red on today's tree **by their own assertion**.

## Rulings (decided, not asked)

1. **The rule is over the stream's honoured root, and the standing reads nothing else.** `HonouredEvents` (what every raw projector is fed) drops, besides the skipped operator-act rows, every `agent.enrolled` row the lifecycle fold skips. The standing then holds, per identity, exactly the root the fold honours. `AgentStandingProjector` is **not** changed and gains no second line of defence: a projector that ignored a second `SuccessfulEnrollment` itself would leave the 500 probes green with the feed's filter deleted, and the filter would be a decoration only the count probes would see (the masked-falsification shape). One site decides; the projector keeps failing loudly on a history the feed should never give it.
2. **Predicate: reuse `broken-link`; add no thirteenth.** A later `agent.enrolled` is decided by steps 1 and 4 as any row is (not an object, or `agent_id` not the stream's: `statement-mismatch`), and then `broken-link`. This is **the verdict both readers already return** for the entry (`Enrolled`: `Count != 0` → `BrokenLink`, after the "not the author's" mismatch), so the reader and the fold agree **without a reader edit**; choosing `statement-mismatch` (G78's precedent) would move three reader sites to keep that agreement, and a new slug would move the closed lists (`LifecyclePredicates.All`, `HonouredFamilyTests.Predicates`, `ReaderVectorAgreementTests`, `KeysReport.cs:193`, `lifecycle.rs`'s `predicate` module, R6.64's list and R11.96's words) for a line an owner reads the same either way. **The vacuity hazard** (`broken-link` now names two kinds of cause) **is discriminated by the type:** no other row of type `agent.enrolled` is ever skipped `broken-link` (a first root fails by `statement-mismatch`), and SRS0 to SRS4 pin the pair (type, predicate). Alternative recorded: a `second-root` slug, if CRS-a shows an agent cannot act on the line (a lead, not built).
3. **The decision is one function.** `EnrolmentRow.Predicate(appended, agent, isFirst)` (internal, `Curia.Application.Credentials`): `statement-mismatch` when the payload is not an object or its `agent_id` is not `agent`; else null for the first, `broken-link` for any later. `LifecycleFold.Enrol` and the later-root branch call it; `BuildHonoured` calls it with `isFirst` from a per-aggregate seen-set. It takes no signature and costs a hash lookup per `agent.enrolled` row, so it is safe on the feed that every request builds (the lifecycle fold's signature checks are not run for it).

## The errata entry (G80), proposed text

Appended **after G79** in the errata, status "specified; not built" until Task 8. Pasted by Task 1 after the controller's immediate re-read of the file. The Status paragraph is written from the run by Task 8, its shape G79's.

> ## G80 — A second root is skipped and counted, and the standing reads only the root the fold honours: an `agent.enrolled` row that is not its stream's honoured root moves no tier, no tenure and no request, whichever stream holds it
>
> **Location.** R4.40 (what the Forum decides from honoured rows), R6.64 (the Forum's fold: "a later `agent.enrolled` is not a row"), R6.54 (the readers' fold), R4.31 (what a repeat registers), R4.21 and R7.7 (the credential history and the live tier), R5.24 (the fold and the consumers answer different questions); errata G70 non-property (a) and Decisions 3, G79 non-property (b), G16; register item 299 (closed), item 293 (annotated).
>
> **How it surfaced.** Filed by PR N2 as a working label (Amendment 1, ruling 19): one forged `agent.enrolled` row in a genuine agent's stream answers its token request `500 server_error` while its re-announcement answers `201` unchanged (OAS39 clause (i)). Ordered by the ordering ruling of 2026-11-24 beside R4.11, and held out of G79 because the enrolment route already refuses a second root and this closure is a change to a fold and its corpora. This plan's reading found that the lifecycle fold already ignores the row (it counts nothing and reports nothing) and the readers already fail it, that the standing is a separate fold fed by honoured events and keyed by the payload's `agent_id`, and that a row in one stream naming another reaches that agent's standing through the token endpoint's read and not through the enrolment route's [Task 0 S2 and S3 set the final wording of this sentence].
>
> **Argument.** An `agent.enrolled` row is the root of an identity's stream and carries no signature (G70 non-property (a)); the Forum honours the first one that names its own stream's agent and nothing else, and decides the rest by the same procedure it decides every row. A rule that the fold applies and the standing does not is a rule that guards a counter: the fold decided "not a row" while the projection that every request recomputes the tier from folded the row into the agent's credential history, where a second enrolment has no transition, so one row the holder of the database credential can append made every request of the agent it named unreadable (R4.40 says a row that does not verify makes no request unreadable). The remedy is that the standing is folded from the roots the fold honours, which makes the row inert for the standing and visible to the operator and the agent: skipped, counted, listed, and a reason for the unattended head signer to stop. A row that is not read cannot be re-read differently by a second consumer, so the closure is made in the feed every raw projector is given, once, and not in the projection, where a second line would let the feed's filter be deleted with every probe green.
>
> ### Requirement text
>
> **R4.48** The Forum SHALL read an identity's credential history, its enrolment instant and its tier (R4.21, R7.7) only from the `agent.enrolled` row that R6.64 honours as the root of that identity's own stream, and SHALL skip and count (R4.40) every other `agent.enrolled` row: one that is not the first `agent.enrolled` of its stream (whether or not the first was honoured), one whose payload is not an object or whose `agent_id` is not its stream's agent, and so one that sits in one stream and names another agent. A skipped enrolment row SHALL NOT date or redate a tenure, SHALL NOT add a credential transition to the agent it names or to any agent, and SHALL NOT make a request of any identity unreadable; it SHALL be reported as every skipped row is (R4.41, R4.40's start-up report and audit, R6.63's stop). The reason: the root is unsigned, so the Forum honours the first that names its own stream and nothing a later row says; a row the fold has decided is not a root and a consumer reads anyway is a second decision made by a second fold, and the credential-holder's one row then locks out an agent or backdates its tenure from a stream the agent does not own.
>
> **R6.64 (rev.)** The sentence "a later `agent.enrolled` is not a row and binds nothing" (and its repetition in the root's paragraph) reads: "a later `agent.enrolled` of the stream is a row, decided by steps 1 and 4 as any row is (`statement-mismatch`) and then skipped, `broken-link`, whether the stream's first enrolment was honoured or was itself skipped; it binds nothing, it is a lifecycle entry for the position of the enrolment's pair (R4.38) as before, and it is never the previous honoured entry of a later row, so a skipped later enrolment moves nothing a later row is decided against". No predicate is added: the closed set stays twelve.
>
> **R6.54 (add.)** A reader's fold SHALL fail the lifecycle at an `agent.enrolled` entry that is not its chain's first, `broken-link` (`statement-mismatch` when its payload is not an object or its `agent_id` is not the author's, which is decided first). This is the readers' behaviour before this entry; the Forum no longer serves such an entry (R4.48), and the vectors that pin it are `conformance/lifecycle/`'s.
>
> **R4.40 (add.)** The decisions R4.40 says are made from honoured rows alone gain: every identity's credential history, enrolment instant and tier (R4.48).
>
> **R4.31 (annotation, no new text).** A repeat registers no second `agent.enrolled` row (R4.31, G14, G79); R4.48 is the rule for the row that something other than the route wrote.
>
> ### Non-properties (stated so that no later reading takes them for guarantees)
>
> (a) **The first planted root still wins.** An `agent.enrolled` row the credential writes at the head of an identity's own stream is honoured: a backdated root still buys the tenure and a root planted before an identity enrols still squats it (register item 293; the closure is a signed enrolment, which G79 did not build). This entry ends the row's reach **across** streams and **after** the first, and no more. (b) **A forged second root now stops the unattended head signer.** It is a row the Forum does not honour, so `sign-head --if-grown` withholds (exit 6) while it follows the latest head, as for every skipped row (R6.63); one appended row can stall the 5-minute timer until the operator audits and signs a head by hand. This is the stop's existing price, newly reachable by a row type that was silent. (c) **The agent a row in another stream names is not told** (G68 non-property (e)): the row is reported on the stream it sits in. (d) **A hand-written second root between an enrolment's root and its pair** makes the pair not the enrolment's pair (`broken-link`) and leaves the key unbound; the route cannot write it (one append holds both) and R6.64 keeps counting a later root as a lifecycle entry. (e) **`broken-link` names two causes** for a row of its kinds; the type distinguishes them, and the SKIPPED line does not say which root was the first (item for the cold read, CRS-a). (f) **A forged `post.accepted`, `answer.accepted` and flag row still move budgets and tier** (items 298, 304); this entry is about the root. > ## G80 — A second root is skipped and counted, and the standing reads only the root the fold honours: an `agent.enrolled` row that is not its stream's honoured root moves no tier, no tenure and no request, whichever stream holds it
>
> **Location.** R4.40 (what the Forum decides from honoured rows), R6.64 (the Forum's fold: "a later `agent.enrolled` is not a row"), R6.54 (the readers' fold), R4.31 (what a repeat registers), R4.21 and R7.7 (the credential history and the live tier), R5.24 (the fold and the consumers answer different questions); errata G70 non-property (a) and Decisions 3, G79 non-property (b), G16; register item 299 (closed), item 293 (annotated).
>
> **How it surfaced.** Filed by PR N2 as a working label (Amendment 1, ruling 19): one forged `agent.enrolled` row in a genuine agent's stream answers its token request `500 server_error` while its re-announcement answers `201` unchanged (OAS39 clause (i)). Ordered by the ordering ruling of 2026-11-24 beside R4.11, and held out of G79 because the enrolment route already refuses a second root and this closure is a change to a fold and its corpora. Reading for the plan found that the lifecycle fold ignores a later `agent.enrolled` of an enrolled stream without counting or reporting it, that the readers already fail it, that the standing is a separate fold fed by honoured events and keyed by the payload's `agent_id`, and that a row in one stream naming another agent therefore reaches that agent's standing through the token endpoint's read and not through the enrolment route's. Task 0 (2026-11-25) measured each on a real Forum and database: the harm reaches **every authenticated request** and not the token only (a token minted before the row gets `500` on the budget, inbox, flags and submit routes; public reads answer `200`); a row in another identity's stream locks out the identity it names, **including one that has not yet enrolled** (its enrolment answers `201` and its token then answers `500` for good, with nothing at the route to show it); the dictionary's enrolment instant takes the forged row's instant, which no tier carries because the same row makes the posture unreadable; and the fold counts such a row only when it is the first row of a fresh stream, so the cross-stream row in an enrolled stream, like the second root in the agent's own, was reported by nothing and let `sign-head --if-grown` sign.
>
> **Argument.** An `agent.enrolled` row is the root of an identity's stream and carries no signature (G70 non-property (a)); the Forum honours the first one that names its own stream's agent and nothing else. The lifecycle fold decided a row of that type three ways by where it stood, not by one rule: the first row naming its own stream's agent was honoured; the first row of a fresh stream naming another agent was skipped and counted; and every later row, whoever it named, was passed over without a word. The standing, a different fold keyed by the payload's agent and not by the stream, took all three kinds alike. A rule that the fold applies in one case and passes over in another, and that the projection every request recomputes the tier from does not apply at all, is a rule that guards a counter at best: the later row was folded into the named agent's credential history, where a second enrolment has no transition, so one row the holder of the database credential can append made every request of the agent it named unreadable, from the agent's own stream or from any other (R4.40 says a row that does not verify makes no request unreadable), and nothing reported it. The remedy is that the standing is folded from the roots the fold honours, and that the fold decides every `agent.enrolled` row by the same steps as every other row: the row becomes inert for the standing and visible to the operator and the agent: skipped, counted, listed, and a reason for the unattended head signer to stop. A row that is not read cannot be re-read differently by a second consumer, so the closure is made in the feed every raw projector is given, once, and not in the projection, where a second line would let the feed's filter be deleted with every probe green.
>
> ### Requirement text
>
> **R4.48** The Forum SHALL read an identity's credential history, its enrolment instant and its tier (R4.21, R7.7) only from the `agent.enrolled` row that R6.64 honours as the root of that identity's own stream, and SHALL skip and count (R4.40) every other `agent.enrolled` row: one that is not the first `agent.enrolled` of its stream (whether or not the first was honoured), one whose payload is not an object or whose `agent_id` is not its stream's agent, and so one that sits in one stream and names another agent, whether or not the agent it names has enrolled. A skipped enrolment row SHALL NOT date or redate a tenure, SHALL NOT add a credential transition to the agent it names or to any agent, and SHALL NOT make a request of any identity unreadable; it SHALL be reported as every skipped row is (R4.41, R4.40's start-up report and audit, R6.63's stop). The reason: the root is unsigned, so the Forum honours the first that names its own stream and nothing a later row says; a row the fold has decided is not a root and a consumer reads anyway is a second decision made by a second fold, and the credential-holder's one row then locks out an agent, existing or not yet enrolled, from a stream the agent does not own.
>
> **R6.64 (rev.)** The sentence "a later `agent.enrolled` is not a row and binds nothing" (and its repetition in the root's paragraph) reads: "a later `agent.enrolled` of the stream is a row, decided by steps 1 and 4 as any row is (`statement-mismatch`) and then skipped, `broken-link`, whether the stream's first enrolment was honoured or was itself skipped; it binds nothing, it is a lifecycle entry for the position of the enrolment's pair (R4.38) as before, and it is never the previous honoured entry of a later row, so a skipped later enrolment moves nothing a later row is decided against". > **R4.31 (annotation, no new text).** A repeat registers no second `agent.enrolled` row (R4.31, G14, G79); R4.48 is the rule for the row that something other than the route wrote.
