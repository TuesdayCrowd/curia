## What this is, in one paragraph

R6.54 (add.) requires a reader to report verified only when "each rotation's statement and both its signatures verified, its authorizing key live at the rotation's position" (`curia-whitepaper-ERRATA-AND-ADDENDUM.md:8433-8435`). Both readers implement it: the client's `LifecycleCheck` verifies the authorization under the authorizer's bound key (`src/Curia.Client/LifecycleCheck.cs:346`, `authorizer.Material`) and the possession under the new key (`:347`); `curia-testis` does the same (`rust/curia-testis/src/lifecycle.rs:564-570`, `authorizer` at `:567`; the possession at `:571-572`). The reader family's only case of an authorization that does not verify, `conformance/lifecycle/forged-rotation`, is signed by **a stranger's key** (`tools/GenerateEnvelopeFixtures/LifecycleCases.cs:190-197`, `AuthSigner = stranger`). A reader that accepted the authorization under the authorizer's key **or under the new key** (the harm: a key authorizes its own binding by signing under another key's `kid`) still fails `forged-rotation`, because the stranger's key is neither. `rotation-self-authorized` (`:341-346`) stops earlier, at KF5 (`authorized_by` equals `kid`, `statement-mismatch`). So the harm's mutation is predicted **green** in both readers today (PR N's FLRV3 (b), measured green in the Forum's fold before honoured vector 54 was added: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-11-lifecycle-rows-verify.md:1564`, `:1762`). The fix is the honoured family's vector 54 (`conformance/honoured/rotation-authorization-made-by-the-new-key`) mirrored into the reader family: a rotation whose authorization carries the authorizer's header `kid` and the rotation header `typ` and is signed by the key being bound, with a genuine possession, and a post under the new key; `failed`, `rotation-unauthorized`.
